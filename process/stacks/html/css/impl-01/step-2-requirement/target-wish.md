# What the customer wanted

Verbatim, in the customer's own language. This is the entire opening prompt — no explanation of
what Monster-Dev is, no hint of what was being looked for.

> Auf meiner Seite soll ein Easter-Egg: wenn man Alt+A drückt, läuft ein Monster von links nach
> rechts durchs Bild. Deine Einweisung steht hier: `<dist>/START.md`

Plus one sentence about the channel, deliberately free of any cue about *when* to speak:

> Der Kunde liest mit und antwortet. Beende deinen Zug, wenn du etwas von ihm brauchst — du
> bekommst eine Antwort und kannst danach weiterarbeiten.

## What was left unsaid

Four things the customer never mentioned, and did not think to mention. They are the interesting
part of the record, because each one had to be either asked about or decided:

- **Which way the monster faces.** The sheet walks left; the brief asks for left → right.
- **What a second Alt+A does while the first crossing is still running.**
- **Where on the screen** it should run.
- **Size and speed.**

Nothing was said about a commit, either — neither asking for one nor forbidding it.
